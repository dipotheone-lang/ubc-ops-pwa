# United Brothers Co. — Approved Vendor List (AVL) Outreach Playbook

**Objective:** Get United Brothers Co. registered on the Approved Vendor List / pre-qualified bidder roster of every target account, then convert registration into tender invitations for civil maintenance, piping shutdowns, steel fabrication, and mechanical installation packages.

**Core principle for Egypt:** Industrial procurement teams don't buy from cold pitches — they buy from *registered vendors* when a requisition lands on their desk. The entire cadence below is framed as a **vendor registration workflow**, not a sales pitch. You are asking for their *process*, which is a request they are culturally and professionally comfortable saying yes to.

---

## 1. The "Vendor Registration" Script — LinkedIn

**Target persona:** Procurement Specialist / Supply Chain Specialist / Contracts Engineer / Tendering Officer at the plant (not HQ marketing staff).

**Connection request note (300-char limit):**

> Dear [First Name], I manage vendor relations at United Brothers Co. — industrial civil works, steel fabrication & piping (clients incl. multinational energy & building-materials producers). I'd appreciate connecting regarding [Company]'s vendor registration process. Thank you.

**Follow-up message (after connection is accepted — send within 24h):**

> Dear [First Name],
>
> Thank you for connecting. I'll keep this brief out of respect for your time.
>
> **United Brothers Co.** is an Egyptian industrial contractor specialized in:
> - Industrial civil works & rapid civil maintenance (live-plant environments)
> - Steel structure fabrication & erection
> - Piping prefabrication, erection & shutdown/turnaround support
> - Mechanical equipment installation
>
> We currently execute works for major industrial operators in Egypt and would like to be **registered on [Company]'s Approved Vendor List** so we can be considered in your upcoming maintenance and project tenders.
>
> Could you kindly share:
> 1. The link to your **vendor registration portal**, or
> 2. Your **pre-qualification criteria / vendor registration form**, and the email address to submit our company profile and pre-qualification documents?
>
> I have our full capability statement, commercial registration, tax card, and HSE/QA documentation ready to submit in whatever format your system requires.
>
> Best regards,
> [Name] — [Title], United Brothers Co.
> [Mobile / WhatsApp] | [email] | [website]

**Why this works in Egypt:** It asks the procurement person to do the *easiest possible action in their job description* (share the registration process), positions UBC as document-ready (a major filter — most local contractors stall at paperwork), and name-drops the multinational client base without violating confidentiality.

## 2. The Technical Follow-Up Email

**Target persona:** Technical Office Manager / Plant Director / Maintenance Manager / Engineering Manager.
**Attachment:** UBC Capability Statement (PDF, max 8–10 pages: org profile, services, HSE stats, equipment list, key reference projects, certifications).

**Subject line options (pick per account):**
- `Vendor Pre-Qualification — Civil & Mechanical Maintenance Contractor (United Brothers Co.)`
- `Shutdown & Turnaround Support — Piping / Steel Fabrication — Pre-Qualification Request`
- `[Plant name] — registered contractor for rapid civil maintenance works`

**Body:**

> Dear Eng. [Last Name],
>
> I am writing to introduce **United Brothers Co.**, an Egyptian industrial contractor serving major operators in the energy, cement, glass, and chemical sectors, and to request the opportunity to **pre-qualify as an approved contractor** for [Company]'s [plant location] facility.
>
> Where we typically add value for plants like yours:
>
> - **Rapid civil maintenance in live plants** — concrete repair, foundations, trenches, bunds, and structural rehabilitation executed under permit-to-work systems without disrupting operations.
> - **Shutdown & turnaround piping** — prefabrication ahead of the window, certified welders (WPS/PQR documentation available), hydrotesting, and tie-ins delivered inside compressed shutdown schedules.
> - **Steel fabrication & erection to code** — pipe racks, platforms, equipment structures, and repairs fabricated in our workshop with full material traceability and QA/QC dossiers.
> - **Mechanical installation** — equipment setting, alignment, and packaged-unit erection.
>
> Our clients include multinational producers in power, building materials, and chemicals in Egypt; reference letters are available upon request. Please find our **capability statement attached**.
>
> Could you advise the correct procedure — or the responsible colleague in procurement/tendering — for adding United Brothers Co. to your approved contractors list? We are ready to submit all pre-qualification documents and to host a technical visit to our workshop, or visit your site, at your convenience.
>
> Respectfully,
> [Name] — [Title]
> United Brothers Co. — Industrial Civil Works | Steel Fabrication | Piping | Mechanical Installation
> [Mobile / WhatsApp] | [email] | [website] | [workshop location]

**Egypt-specific notes:**
- Always address engineers as **"Eng. [Name]"** (م. / مهندس) — omitting the title reads as disrespectful.
- Send **Sunday–Thursday, 9:00–13:00**; avoid Friday/Saturday entirely and post-14:00 during Ramadan.
- Keep the attachment **under 5 MB** — plant mail servers routinely block larger files.
- The email asks the technical leader to *route you to procurement* — in Egyptian plants a technical referral into procurement carries far more weight than approaching procurement cold.

## 3. The Gatekeeper Phone Script (Receptionist → Procurement Email)

**Goal:** Obtain the direct email (and ideally name) of the procurement/tendering department. Never try to sell to the gatekeeper.

**Arabic (primary — use with all local plants):**

> السلام عليكم، معاك [الاسم] من شركة United Brothers للمقاولات الصناعية.
> لو سمحت، إحنا عايزين نقدّم ملف تسجيل موردين للشركة — أعمال مدنية صناعية وتصنيع حديد ومواسير.
> ممكن حضرتك تدّيني الإيميل الرسمي بتاع **إدارة المشتريات أو المناقصات** عشان نبعت عليه ملف الشركة والمستندات؟
> *(لو سألت ليه:)* ده ملف تأهيل مسبق — تسجيل في قايمة الموردين المعتمدين، مش عرض سعر.
> *(قبل الإنهاء:)* وممكن أعرف اسم المهندس أو الأستاذ المسؤول عن تسجيل الموردين؟ … شكراً جداً لحضرتك.

**English equivalent (for multinationals / English-speaking reception):**

> Good morning, this is [Name] from United Brothers Co., an industrial contracting company.
> We'd like to submit our **vendor registration file** — industrial civil works, steel fabrication, and piping.
> Could you kindly give me the official email of your **procurement or tendering department** so we can send our company profile and pre-qualification documents?
> *(If asked why:)* It's a pre-qualification file for your approved vendor list — not a quotation.
> *(Before ending:)* And may I have the name of the person responsible for vendor registration? … Thank you very much.

**Gatekeeper tactics that work in Egypt:**
- Framing as "تسجيل موردين" (vendor registration) is *administrative*, not sales — receptionists route paperwork requests without resistance.
- Ask for the **email first, name second** — the email is low-stakes for them to give; the name feels like a bigger favor, so sequence it after they've already said yes once.
- If routed to a generic `info@` address, send there **and** call back 3 days later: *"We submitted our vendor file to info@ — could you confirm the procurement department received it, or share their direct address so I can resend?"* This second call almost always yields `procurement@` / `tenders@`.
- Best calling window: **Sunday–Thursday, 10:00–13:00** (after the morning meeting rush, before lunch/prayer break).

## 4. The 14-Day Cadence Workflow (per target account)

Run **two parallel threads** per account: PROC (procurement/supply-chain contact) and TECH (technical office / plant management). One SDR can run ~25 accounts simultaneously at this cadence.

| Day | Channel | Persona | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Phone | Gatekeeper | Call reception → obtain procurement/tendering email + responsible person's name (Script 3). Log in CRM. |
| **1** | LinkedIn | PROC | Send connection request with note (Script 1) to 1–2 procurement/supply-chain profiles at the plant. |
| **2** | Email | PROC | Send vendor-registration email to the address obtained on Day 1, attaching capability statement + document checklist (CR, tax card, ISO/HSE certs). |
| **3** | LinkedIn | PROC | If connected: send full registration message (Script 1 follow-up). If not: send request to one alternate procurement profile. |
| **4** | LinkedIn | TECH | Connection request to Technical Office Manager / Maintenance Manager with a technical-flavored note (shutdown piping, rapid civil maintenance). |
| **5** | Email | TECH | Send Technical Follow-Up Email (Script 2) with capability statement. Reference the vendor file already submitted to procurement ("our file is with your procurement team"). |
| **7** | Phone | PROC | Call procurement directly: *"We submitted our pre-qualification file on [date] — could you confirm it was received, and is anything missing for vendor registration?"* (Confirmation framing, not selling.) |
| **8** | LinkedIn | PROC + TECH | Light-touch engagement: like/comment substantively on the company's or contact's recent post (plant milestone, expansion news). Keeps you visible without pestering. |
| **9** | Email | PROC | Short bump reply *on the same thread* (keeps context): "Following up on our vendor registration file — happy to resend any document or complete your standard registration form." |
| **10** | LinkedIn | TECH | Follow-up message to technical contact: offer a **15-minute capability call or workshop visit**; mention one specific relevant scope (e.g., "we prefabricate spools ahead of shutdown windows to cut on-site hot work"). |
| **11** | Phone | TECH | Call the technical office (number often obtainable from reception on Day 1). Goal: a site meeting or an invitation to the next maintenance tender. |
| **12** | Email | PROC | Send **one-page reference-project sheet** (photos + scope + client sector, no confidential data) as a value-add: "For your vendor file — recent shutdown and fabrication references." |
| **14** | Email + CRM | Both | **Break-up / park email:** "We understand registration cycles take time — our file remains valid and we'll check back next quarter. For any urgent civil/piping/steel requirement, my mobile is below." → Set CRM task to re-touch in 45 days, and flag account for the quarterly "tender-season" re-activation wave. |

### Cadence rules
1. **Never send the same text on two channels the same day** — Egyptian procurement staff talk to each other; duplicated copy reads as automation.
2. **Every touch references the registration file**, not "checking in." The file gives each follow-up a legitimate administrative pretext.
3. **WhatsApp discipline:** once a contact replies on email/LinkedIn and shares a mobile number, move to WhatsApp — it is the de-facto B2B channel in Egypt — but *never* cold-WhatsApp a number you weren't given.
4. **Log the AVL status per account** in CRM with stages: `No contact → Email obtained → File submitted → File confirmed received → Registered/AVL → Invited to tender → Bid submitted`. The KPI for the SDR is **files confirmed received**, not meetings booked.
5. **Quarterly re-activation:** state-affiliated plants (petroleum sector especially) refresh vendor rosters around budget cycles (July fiscal year start) — re-touch every parked account in June and December.
6. **Ramadan adjustment:** compress calling windows to 10:00–13:30, expect 2× slower responses, and shift the Day-14 break-up to Day 21.
